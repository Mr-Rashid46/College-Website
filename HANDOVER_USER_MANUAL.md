# NKTT College CMS - Non-Technical Staff User Manual

Welcome to the **N. K. T. Thanawala College Content Management System (CMS)**. This guide is written specifically for college non-technical administrative staff, department heads, and exam section editors.

---

## 1. Logging In to the CMS

1. Open your web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Firefox).
2. Navigate to: `https://your-college-domain.edu.in/admin/login`
3. Enter your assigned official email address and password.
4. Click **Log In to Admin Panel**.

---

## 2. Managing Notices & Examination Circulars

### Adding a New Notice
1. From the left sidebar, click **Notices & Circulars**.
2. Click the **Publish New Notice** button at the top right.
3. Fill in the following details:
   - **Notice Title**: Type a clear description (e.g. *F.Y.B.Com Semester II Examination Timetable April 2026*).
   - **Category**: Select *Admission*, *Exam*, *General*, or *Circular*.
   - **Publish Date & Time**: Set the date and time when the notice should appear live.
   - **Status**: Choose `Published` to make it live, or `Draft` to save it privately.
   - **Notice Attachment**: Click *Choose from Media Library* or upload a PDF document.
   - **Ticker Flag**: Check the box if you want this notice to scroll in the urgent homepage ticker.
4. Click **Save Notice**.

### Bulk Actions (Deleting or Publishing Multiple Notices)
1. Tick the checkboxes next to the notices you wish to modify.
2. Click **Publish All**, **Unpublish All**, or **Delete Selected** from the blue toolbar at the top.

---

## 3. Uploading & Using the Media Library

The **Media Library** allows you to upload logos, event photos, and PDF files once and reuse them across multiple pages or notices.

1. Click **Media Library** from the top header or left sidebar.
2. Under **Upload New Asset**, select your image or PDF.
3. Type a descriptive **Alt Text** (e.g. *Annual Sports Day Group Photo*). This ensures accessibility compliance.
4. Click **Upload Asset**.
5. To copy an asset link to paste into a page or email, click the **Copy Link** icon on any asset card.

---

## 4. Editing Static Web Pages & Admissions Information

1. Click **Static Pages** from the left sidebar.
2. Locate the target page (e.g. *About Us*, *Admissions*, *IQAC*).
3. Click the **Edit** pencil icon.
4. Update the content using the Rich Text Editor.
5. Fill in the **Meta Title** and **Meta Description** for search engines.
6. Click **Save Page**.

---

## 5. Checking Contact Form Submissions

1. Click **Contact Inquiries** in the sidebar.
2. View incoming queries from prospective students and parents.
3. Click any submission to view contact numbers, email addresses, and detailed inquiries.
4. Click **Mark as Read** to keep the inbox organized.

---

## 6. Audit Activity Logs

If you need to verify who made a change or deleted a notice:
1. Click **Audit Activity Logs** under Access Control.
2. Filter by resource (e.g., *Notice*, *User*, *Media*) or search by action name.
3. Review exact timestamps, user accounts, and IP addresses.

---

## 7. Security Best Practices

- Always click **Logout** before closing your browser when using shared campus computers.
- Never share superadmin credentials over SMS or WhatsApp.
- Maintain strong passwords with at least 8 characters, including numbers and uppercase letters.
